= Lab 07 — Ask your own logs

[cols="1,3"]
|===
| Product   | {rhoai}, OpenShift Logging (Loki), Granite
| Time      | About 5 minutes

| Outcome   | An on-cluster notebook over your own operational data, and the MTTR case for it
|===

== Objective

Aim the <<lab-04>> retrieval pattern at operations itself. Cluster logs,
runbooks, incident history and change records become a private notebook you
can question during a live incident — a NotebookLM for the platform, running
on the platform. Then read the numbers: the payoff is measured in mean time to
restore, because diagnosis is where the hours live.

== Why this matters more than it looks

When something breaks at 2 a.m., the answer usually already exists — in a
runbook, a past incident's postmortem, a change record from that afternoon, or
a log line from the failing pod. What costs hours is that those four things
live in four systems and three people's heads. A notebook grounded in all of
them answers the on-call question — *what changed, has this happened before,
which runbook applies* — in seconds, with citations the engineer can check
before acting.

Two properties make this defensible in a tax agency:

* *It is the same stack as the taxpayer-facing system.* Nothing new is
  installed; only the corpus changes. One retrieval pattern, two corpora, one
  governance story.
* *It stays inside the boundary.* Logs carry hostnames, account fragments and
  case identifiers — FTI-adjacent by nature. The notebook runs on the same
  OpenShift install it observes; nothing is exported to a hosted service.

== Prerequisites

For live mode: an OpenShift cluster with OpenShift Logging (Loki), the
<<lab-04>> vector store and model serving in place, `logcli`, and Python for
the ingest and reporting helpers.

cd labs/lab07-ops-notebook

== Walkthrough

[cols="1,2,3", options="header"]
|===
| Step | Command | What to look for

| 1
| `logcli query '{namespace="{ns}"} \|= "ERROR"'`
| The incident: OOM errors and restart loops on the predictor pods. This is
  the raw material the notebook will read.

| 2
| `python3 ingest_ops.py`
| Four corpora going into one index: Loki export, runbooks, postmortems,
  change records. Same vector store as <<lab-04>> — only the corpus changed.

| 3
| `llama-stack-client inference chat-completion ...`
| A cited diagnosis: the change record that caused it, the past incident that
  matches it, and the runbook section that fixes it. Verify, then act.

| 4
| `awx job_templates launch ...`
| Remediation through the <<lab-05>> automation, so the fix lands in the
  controller's job history — that list is your evidence trail.

| 5
| `python3 mttr_report.py`
| The decomposition. Detect, fix and verify are unchanged; diagnosis falls
  from hours to minutes. That is the whole argument.
|===

== What good looks like

The diagnosis names a specific change record, a specific precedent incident
and a specific runbook section, each with a retrieval score — and the on-call
engineer can open all three before acting. If answers come back without
citations, or citations that do not support the claim, treat that exactly as
you would in <<lab-04>>: the retrieval threshold or the corpus needs work, and
the model's fluency is not a substitute.

The representative MTTR decomposition:

[cols="2,1,1,1,1,1", options="header"]
|===
| | Detect | Diagnose | Fix | Verify | Total

| Four quarters before | 4m | 2h 41m | 22m | 9m | 3h 16m
| With the notebook    | 4m | 24m    | 22m | 9m | 59m
|===

IMPORTANT: Generated incident summaries are draft artifacts. They become
operations knowledge for the next event only after the same curation and
review that any other knowledge record gets — do not let fluency become
authority.

== Troubleshooting

`logcli` returns nothing::
Check the tenant header and that the LokiStack gateway route is reachable
from the workbench. `oc -n openshift-logging get lokistack` first.

The notebook answers without citations::
Same failure mode as <<lab-04>>: the retrieval score threshold is too low or
the corpus is missing the relevant material. Ingest the runbooks before you
trust the answers.

Ingest is slow or huge::
Summarize log windows before embedding rather than embedding raw lines — the
notebook needs the shape of the incident, not every log line ever written.
