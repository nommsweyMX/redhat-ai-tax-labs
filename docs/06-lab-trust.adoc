= Lab 06 — Prove it to your ISSO

[cols="1,3"]
|===
| Product   | {rhel}, Compliance Operator, TrustyAI, Sigstore

| Outcome   | The four checks an authorizing official actually runs, with real results

== Objective

Walk the exact checks an authorizing official will run against an AI workload:
is this the model we approved, is the cryptography validated, is the platform
still hardened this quarter, and is the model behaving the way it did at
accreditation.

Every one of these is a platform feature you switch on. None of them is a
project you fund.

== Prerequisites

For live mode: `oc` authenticated to a cluster with the Compliance Operator and
TrustyAI installed, `cosign` on the path, the agency signing public key, and
SSH access to a GPU node.

[source,bash]
----
cd labs/lab06-trust
LAB_PACE=step ./run.sh
----

== Walkthrough

[cols="1,2,3", options="header"]
|===
| Step | Check | What to look for

| 1
| `cosign verify`
| The signature validates and names the signer, the timestamp and the change
  record. Verification happens *before admission*, not as an audit afterwards.

| 2
| `fips-mode-setup --check` and `sestatus`
| FIPS enabled, SELinux enforcing. Both are supported operating states, not
  hardening you bolt on.

| 3
| `oc get compliancescan`
| A scan result — including `NON-COMPLIANT`, which is the honest outcome.

| 4
| `oc get compliancecheckresult ... FAIL`
| Exactly which control failed, with a remediation available.

| 5
| `TrustyAI metrics`
| Drift and fairness figures on live inference traffic, compared against the
  accreditation baseline.
|===

== Why the failing check is in the lab on purpose

Step 3 returns `NON-COMPLIANT` for the `ocp4-moderate` profile, and step 4
shows that exactly one of 217 checks failed: audit log forwarding.

A demo where every scan passes teaches the wrong lesson. Real clusters have
findings, and the useful skill is reading a result and deciding what to do
about it. There are two ways to fix this one — apply the remediation directly,
or let the <<lab-05>> rulebook apply it, which produces the same fix plus a
job record with a launching identity. The second is the one your assessor
wants.

== Reading the drift numbers correctly

The TrustyAI output shows `channel_mix` drift at 0.212 against a 0.150
threshold. The instinct is to conclude the model is failing. It is not.

Input distribution shifted because more taxpayers moved to the portal. Nothing
is wrong with the model at all — drift monitoring is telling you that the
*world* changed, which is exactly what you want it to tell you. The response is
to investigate the input, decide whether the accreditation baseline is still
representative, and re-baseline deliberately if it is not.

Confusing "the inputs changed" with "the model broke" is the most common
misreading of these metrics, and it leads teams to retrain when they should
have been asking a business question.

== The four alerts worth wiring

`labs/lab06-trust/manifests/trustyai-service.yaml` defines three Prometheus
rules alongside the TrustyAI service:

`ModelDriftDetected`:: Input distribution has moved from the baseline. Warning,
after 30 minutes — long enough not to fire on a lunchtime spike.

`FairnessOutOfBand`:: Disparate impact ratio outside the agreed band. Critical.
The annotation says to route affected contacts to human review until resolved,
which is an operational decision that belongs in the alert, not in someone's
memory.

`ModelProvenanceMismatch`:: The served digest no longer matches the approved
change record. Critical, and handled by the <<lab-05>> rulebook, which
escalates rather than self-remediating.

== Troubleshooting

`cosign verify` fails on a model you believe is correct::
Check you are verifying by digest rather than tag, and that the public key
matches the signing key used at release.

No compliancescan resources exist::
The Compliance Operator is installed but no `ScanSettingBinding` has been
created. Nothing runs until you bind a profile.

TrustyAI reports no metrics::
It needs inference traffic and a configured baseline. A freshly installed
service with no traffic reports nothing, which looks like a broken install.

FIPS check says disabled::
FIPS mode is set at installation. It is not a runtime toggle, and retrofitting
it is a rebuild.

== Discussion questions

. Who at your agency signs model artifacts today, and where does that key live?
. Which hardening profile applies to this workload, and who accepted the current
  set of findings?
. What is your accreditation baseline for model behaviour, and what process
  re-baselines it when the world legitimately changes?
