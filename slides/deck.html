    AI foundations · no alphabet soup

    Four different jobs get called “AI”

    Treat AI as a toolbox, not a magic employee. Pick the tool by the job you need done.

    Estimate
    Predictive AI
    data → information
    Finds patterns in historical data to score, classify or forecast an outcome.
    Analogy
    A weather forecast: “there is a 70% chance,” not a guarantee.
    Create
    Generative AI / LLM
    information → knowledge
    Produces new language by predicting useful next tokens from patterns learned during training and context supplied now.
    Analogy
    A very well-read drafting assistant — fast, fluent, and still capable of being wrong.
    Reason + act
    Agentic AI
    knowledge → action
    Pairs a model with approved tools, state and rules so it can plan steps and call systems.
    Analogy
    A junior analyst with a badge and a restricted tool belt — permissions still matter.
    Execute
    Automation
    executes judgement
    Runs deterministic, tested steps when a condition or human decision says to act.
    Analogy
    A checklist that actually performs the checklist. This is where Ansible belongs.
    What predictive AI is not: a crystal ball.   What an LLM is not: a database, policy authority or source of truth.   What an agent is not: permission to bypass controls.

    Why it matters

    From data to judgement — and who climbs each step

    Why it matters

    The backlog lives at the bottom of the ladder

    Transcribing, matching, routing and summarizing consume the workforce. Every hour a machine absorbs down there is an hour an examiner spends deciding — the only work that resolves a case.

    Why it matters

    Judgement is deliberately never automated

    The model classifies, grounds and drafts; a named person decides and signs. That is a design rule, not a technical limit — and it is what makes the deployment defensible to counsel and oversight.

    The knowledge flywheel knowledge

    After review, the incident summary, root cause, evidence and successful remediation can become searchable operations knowledge for the next event. Generated content is a draft artifact until it passes the same curation and authority rules as any other knowledge record.

    Example

    “Why is this service slow?”

    Predictive AI says GPU queue depth is abnormal. The LLM explains the evidence. RAG cites the approved saturation runbook. Event-Driven Ansible scales or reroutes after the required approval.

    Boundary

    Do not let fluency become authority

    The LLM can propose a root cause. Telemetry, system state, retrieved source material and deterministic checks establish whether that explanation is actually supported.

    Glossary — the jargon, demystified
    Token

    The small piece of text a model actually reads and writes — roughly four characters. “Form 8822” is three tokens; a page of a notice is about 700.

    Tokenization

    Splitting text into tokens and turning each one into a number — the only translation step between your words and the mathematics.

    Weights (parameters)

    The billions of numbers learned during training. They encode patterns of language, not a database of facts, and they are frozen once deployed.

    Inference

    Running the model: tokens in, one predicted token out, repeated until the answer is complete. Lab 02 is the only lab that changes weights — every model call in the others is inference.

    Prompt

    Everything you send — the instructions plus the text to work on. Sending it is the whole request; no other step exists.

    Context window

    How much the model can consider at once: your prompt plus its own answer so far. Short-term memory, cleared after every request.

    Temperature

    How adventurous the next-token choice is. Near zero the model repeats its best guess reliably — the right setting for tax work.

    Embedding

    Text converted into a vector — a point in space where similar meanings sit close together. What makes searching by meaning possible.

    Vector store

    A database of embeddings. Labs 04 and 07 run one in pgvector so guidance and logs can be found by meaning rather than by keyword.

    RAG — retrieval-augmented generation

    Fetch the relevant passages from your documents, place them into the prompt, and require the answer to cite them.

    Fine-tuning (LoRA)

    Deliberately adjusting weights with your own examples. LoRA trains a small add-on instead of all the weights — cheaper, faster, reversible.

    Hallucination

    A confident answer assembled from plausible tokens with nothing behind it. Countered with retrieval, thresholds, refusal — and a human signature.

    Drift

    The input mix changing compared to the baseline you accredited. It means the world changed — not that the model decayed. Weights cannot rot.
